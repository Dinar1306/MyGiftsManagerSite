Privacy policy

Effective date: 1 September 2026

The My Gifts app stores contacts, occasions and gifts entered by the user only on the user’s device.

The app does not use network communication to transfer this data, contains no advertising and uses no analytics. It is available free of charge.

CSV files are created only when the user explicitly requests it. When the app is deleted, local data may be deleted; it can be restored from a CSV file exported earlier.
